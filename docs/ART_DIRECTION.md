# Art direction

Original 1990s football arcade viewed from behind the striker. Night lights, dark blue grandstands, layered crowd pixels, bottle green turf with perspective bands and chalk lines. Orange goalkeeper is the brightest character; off-white typography and acid lime interface accents frame the pitch. No imported copyrighted assets, emojis or mismatched asset packs.

Palette: ink #0b1418, stands #14252e, teal #24444a, grass #164e3d/#1c6047, chalk #d9eadb, lime #d6fb71, orange #ff903e, shade #bf4a2f, skin #e9ad80, blue striker #578cdb. Discrete pixel clusters, limited palette, hard edges and nearest-neighbor texture sampling. Source characters use 32×48 pixels with a consistent outlined silhouette, boots, gloves, hair, jersey highlights and pose-specific limbs. Stadium contains patterned crowd, floodlight beams, flags, advertising, net and perspective grass detail. UI lives outside canvas; original CSS system font stack avoids external font loading.

Art is reproducibly drawn at runtime from repository source; no asset download failures, bitmap licenses or runtime network calls. Prefer crisp deliberate pixel silhouettes with animation over procedurally noisy blobs. Scene adds shadows, ball trails, impact sparks, animated net and subtle camera motion.

## Chaos expansion

The original orange keeper also has a `keeper-smoke` idle pose: one glove holds a slim ivory cigarette to his mouth, with an amber-red ember at source pixel (30,15); the other hand rests on his hip. Rising pixel smoke is generated by the scene rather than baked into the texture. Reduced motion reduces decorative particles.

Giant saves preserve the keeper's silhouette and palette. The scene scales him continuously from his normal scale toward 7.5 during the dive and back during landing. The glove-to-ball contact transform must include this changing scale, with no teleportation or detached impact.

The invading supporter is distinct from the blue striker: magenta shirt #b2588b, plum shadows #763e68, lime scarf #d6ef7f/#c7e66d, brown hair, casual shorts, bare legs and ivory sneakers. `fan-run` and `fan-kick` are original 32×48 textures facing away toward the goal, with origin (16,48). The kick toe shares the striker's contact anchor (23,45); the trailing scarf makes the unexpected entrance legible during movement. Fan celebration and exit are animated by the scene.

Outside-frame aim uses an orange reticle. A miss travels visibly wide, high or onto the turf and displays WIDE; it does not trigger the net's goal reaction. The UI includes a dedicated MISSES counter, using the same typography and spacing as existing statistics.
